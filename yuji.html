Yuji Itadori 虎杖悠仁
The Strongest Sorcerer
Intro
Born in Sendai on March 20, 2003. Yuji received his education in his hometown before being transferred by Satoru Gojo to Tokyo Jujutsu-Tech.
History
Yuji becomes the vessel of Sukuna Ryomen and becomes a sorcerer with an extremely powerful physique. During the battle against Sukuna, he is able to continuously use black flashes to pierce Sukuna's soul and secure victory.
Wishes
Yuji only wants peace and to die surrounded by his friends, sadly. Yuji is a half-cursed human who cannot die.
